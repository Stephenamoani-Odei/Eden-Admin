# EdenPlus Admin Dashboard

Admin dashboard for **EdenPlus Education Consult** — manage programs, clients, payments, admins, and audit history, all in one place.

## About

This dashboard is the operational hub for EdenPlus Education Consult. It replaces manual spreadsheets and paper records with a single system for tracking who's enrolled, who's paid, what's still owed, and a full history of every action taken — so nothing gets lost and nothing has to be tracked from memory.

## Who it's for

Built for EdenPlus staff and administrators who need to:
- Register and manage clients
- Track payments as they come in, including part payments
- Review and approve transactions submitted by clients
- Keep an accurate, exportable record of the business at any point in time

## Features

- **Dashboard** — real-time stats: payments collected, pending, and overdue, at a glance
- **Programs** — add and manage the programs offered, mark them as done, or remove them
- **Payments** — track every payment made, review and approve payments submitted by clients
- **Record payment** — log a payment against a client and program, with full and part payments handled automatically
- **Report and stat** — a full breakdown of who's paid, who's pending, and who's overdue, with exportable reports
- **Admins** — manage who has access to the dashboard, and at what level
- **Audit log** — a permanent, unchangeable record of every action taken in the system, for full accountability

## How it works

Admins sign in with a secure login to access the dashboard. From there, every action — adding a client, recording a payment, approving a transaction, managing another admin's access — is tracked automatically in the audit log. Nothing is ever recorded silently, and nothing in the audit history can be edited or deleted after the fact.

## Security

Access is restricted to authorized administrators only. Every action is logged, and sensitive operations require the appropriate admin permission level. The system is designed so that no single point of failure can compromise client or payment data.
